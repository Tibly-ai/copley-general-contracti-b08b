Structural Repairs: Stabilizing What Storm or Time Has Damaged

When a wall, header, or load-bearing element has been compromised — by storm impact, water intrusion, or age — our structural repair work restores the safety, stability, and integrity of residential and commercial structures. This covers framing and headers through full load-bearing corrections, and it runs through the same licensed hands (CBC1263276) that handle our custom builds and renovations, so nothing about the repair is a guess.

Mold Remediation: Licensed Assessment and Removal After Water Damage

Water damage in a Clearwater home — from a storm, a leak, or flooding — often means mold risk within days. Copley carries both the mold assessor license (MRSA3460) and the mold remediation license (MRSR3628), so the same team that identifies the mold can also remove it, rather than you hiring an assessor and a separate remediation contractor. Mold remediation is one of the eight specialty services we run in-house.
