What Emergency Structural Repair Covers

Copley handles the full arc of storm and structural damage response, all under licensed hands:

Structural Assessment & Stabilization

Under FL Contractor License CBC1263276, we evaluate compromised framing, headers, walls, and other structural elements, then stabilize the property so it's safe to occupy or re-enter while repairs are planned.

Mold Assessment

Water intrusion from a storm or plumbing failure often means mold risk within days. Under Assessor License MRSA3460, we inspect and identify the scope of any mold growth before remediation begins.

Mold Remediation

Under Remediator License MRSR3628, our team removes and remediates mold growth — the same crew that assessed the damage, so nothing gets lost in a handoff between separate companies.

Repair & Rebuild

Once the structure is stabilized and any mold issue resolved, we move into the repair or rebuild phase — the same process we use for renovations and structural repair projects across Tampa Bay, with honest pricing and real timelines.
