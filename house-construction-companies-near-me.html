Full-Service House Construction, Renovation & Repair

When people search for a "house construction company near me," they're usually looking for more than just new builds. Copley covers the full lifecycle of a home:

  1. Custom Home Builds — from concept to completion, designed for Florida weather and Florida codes.
  2. Major Renovations — turning outdated spaces into functional, modern environments with honest scopes and careful demolition from day one.
  3. Structural Repairs — framing, headers, and load-bearing corrections that restore safety and stability.
  4. Garage Conversions — offices, guest suites, and studios built out of underused garage space.

  6. Mold Remediation — licensed remediation and assessment work (MRSR3628, MRSA3460) to restore healthy indoor air.
  7. Painting — interior and exterior work with proper prep, color matching, and custom trim.
  8. Government Contracting — construction delivered in compliance with government standards, managed by a dedicated team lead.
