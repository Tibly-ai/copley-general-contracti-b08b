Storm Recovery and Water Damage Work

Dunedin's coastal location means storm damage and water intrusion are real concerns for homeowners here. Copley has direct experience with this type of recovery work: after Hurricane Milton, we remodeled a condo unit with water damage, coordinating closely with the owner to meet a tight deadline so an elderly family member could move back in. In a separate project, we repaired water damage from a broken pipe — replacing drywall, tile, and wood flooring, and matching existing paint, texture, and door casings (custom-built on site) — delivered on the promised three-week timeline and on budget.
