What Affects Your Price

  • Extent of water intrusion — a single wet wall behind a washing machine costs far less to remediate than a whole-room or whole-floor event.
  • Whether structure is compromised — if the assessor finds affected framing or subfloor, the job moves from remediation-only into Copley's Structural Repairs scope, which changes both cost and timeline.
  • Access and location — a first-floor bathroom in St. Petersburg is a different job than a second-story unit or a property further out in the service area.
  • Finish level on the rebuild — once remediation is complete, the cost to restore the space depends on the finishes you choose during the Major Renovation phase.
