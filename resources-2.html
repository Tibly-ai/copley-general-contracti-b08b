What Affects Your Price

Every remodel we price in St. Petersburg comes down to the same handful of variables:

Scope and square footage. A garage conversion into a home office costs differently than the same space converted into a guest suite with plumbing added.

Structural condition. Florida homes — especially older ones near the coast — sometimes reveal framing, header, or load-bearing issues once walls open up. Our free walkthrough is designed to flag this before you sign anything.

Permitting and code requirements. Custom builds and ADUs require permits sized to the work; timelines and costs shift based on what your specific municipality requires.

Finish level. The materials and fixtures you choose move the number more than almost anything else.

Remediation findings. For mold work, our Assessor credential (MRSA3460) means the scope of remediation is based on an actual assessment, not a guess.
