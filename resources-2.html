What Affects Your Price

Every project is different, and your final number depends on factors we walk through during your free, no-pressure walkthrough:

  • Scope and square footage — a single-room renovation costs differently than a whole-home remodel or new ADU.
  • Structural condition — projects that uncover framing, header, or load-bearing issues require corrections before finish work can proceed.
  • Permitting and code requirements — Florida-specific codes and local St. Petersburg permitting timelines affect both cost and schedule.
