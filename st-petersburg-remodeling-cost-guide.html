What Affects Your Price

Every renovation is different, but these are the factors that move the number up or down on almost every project we take on in St. Petersburg:

  • Scope and square footage. A single-room refresh costs less than a whole-home renovation or ground-up custom build.
  • Structural involvement. Projects that touch framing, headers, or load-bearing walls (our Structural Repairs specialty) require engineering review and more labor.
  • Permitting and code compliance. St. Petersburg and Pinellas County permitting timelines and requirements affect both cost and schedule — we build for Florida weather and Florida codes from day one.
  • Site conditions. Older homes, tight lots, or existing damage (including mold, which falls under our Remediator license MRSR3628) can add time and cost.
  • Finish level. The materials and fixtures you choose have the widest swing on final price of any single factor.
  • Timeline. Rushed schedules or work that requires phased permitting can add cost versus a standard timeline.
