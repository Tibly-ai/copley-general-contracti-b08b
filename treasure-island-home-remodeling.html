Home Remodeling in Treasure Island, FL

Treasure Island homes take a beating from salt air, sun, and storms, so a remodel here has to hold up to more than a typical inland renovation. Copley General Contracting is a family-owned, licensed and insured general contractor based in Saint Petersburg, building and repairing homes across Tampa Bay — including Treasure Island — for over 10 years. Whether you're updating a kitchen, rebuilding after storm damage, or converting a garage into livable space, our crews handle the scope from first walkthrough to final coat.

Remodeling Services We Bring to Treasure Island

Copley offers eight specialty services, and most Treasure Island remodeling projects draw on several of them at once:

  • Major Renovations — Comprehensive renovation solutions that turn outdated spaces into functional, modern environments, with honest scopes and careful demolition from day one.
  • Structural Repairs — Repair solutions that restore the safety, stability, and integrity of residential and commercial structures, from framing and headers to load-bearing corrections.
  • Mold Remediation — Safe, effective mold removal to restore healthy indoor environments and prevent future growth. FL Licensed Remediator MRSR3628 and Licensed Assessor MRSA3460.
  • Garage Conversions — Turning underutilized garage space into offices, guest suites, studios, and everything in between.
  • Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs) — Custom-designed secondary living spaces that add value, flexibility, and additional housing options to a property.
  • Painting — Interior and exterior painting with proper prep work, color matching to existing finishes, and custom-made trim and door casings.

If your Treasure Island project touches more than one of these — say, storm repair plus a full kitchen and bath remodel — one call covers the whole scope, because our licenses (contractor, remediator, and assessor) run through the same team.

Custom builds and major renovations at Copley are built for Florida weather, Florida codes, and the way people actually live here — a standard that matters on a barrier island like Treasure Island where storm exposure is part of daily life. Rebuilding after storm damage or reworking a tired space, we treat every renovation as if it were our own: honest scopes, careful demolition, and finishes that hold up to real life.

How the Remodeling Process Works

A free walkthrough starts every project. It takes about 45 minutes: we look at the space, talk through what you want, flag anything structural, and follow up with a written scope and a realistic timeline. No charge and no pressure to sign anything. Bring sketches, photos, or a list of what bugs you about the current space.

From there, our project managers stay on the job through daily updates and on-site supervision, so you know what's happening at every stage — not just at the walkthrough and the final walkthrough.
